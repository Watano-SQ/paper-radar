Scoring behavior added

Weekly report now scores candidates by default.
Scoring is configurable and explainable: metadata, freshness, source quality, lane keyword matches, cross-domain signal, review/survey signal, and noise penalties.
Candidate levels are S_candidate, A_candidate, B_candidate, C_candidate, reject.
These are candidate-pool levels only, not final reading grades.
Added --no-scoring to use the old metadata-ranking compatibility path.
Reject log behavior

Default output: data/reports/rejected_candidates_<YYYY-WW>.md
Reject/downrank log groups by reason and respects reject_log_max_per_reason.
